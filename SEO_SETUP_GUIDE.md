## Next Steps for Google Listing 🚀

### 1. **Submit to Google Search Console** (REQUIRED)
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Add your property: `https://buyorbyeee.vercel.app`
3. Verify ownership (use HTML tag method - easiest for Vercel)
4. Submit your sitemap: `https://buyorbyeee.vercel.app/sitemap.xml`
5. Request indexing for your homepage

**Add verification code:**
```typescript
// In src/app/layout.tsx, uncomment and add your code:
verification: {
  google: 'YOUR_VERIFICATION_CODE_HERE',
},
```

### 2. **Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools** (RECOMMENDED)
